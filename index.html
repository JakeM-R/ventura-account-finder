02 / How it works

Where the list comes from

  • Every Monday a GitHub Action pulls the latest Overture Maps places release (open business listings from Meta, Microsoft, Esri and OpenStreetMap) for all 50 states and DC. The date at the top is the last pull.
  • It keeps places listed as building-supply, lumber or wholesale businesses whose names say they supply something (“Supply”, “Materials”, “Lumber”, “Distribution”). Contractors, installers, landscapers and plumbing, electrical and HVAC suppliers are left out.
  • Each company gets a segment from its name: roofing and siding, gypsum and drywall, lumber, or general building supplies.
  • Branches are grouped into one company when they share a brand, a cleaned-up name or a website domain.
  • Ranking is by branches listed in the chosen state. Listings miss some yards and occasionally misfile one, so treat it as a strong starting list, not a census.
  • Companies on the ventura.ai customer logo wall are tagged and left out of the CSV. National distributors (ABC Supply, Beacon, SRS, L&W, GMS and similar) are hidden unless you ask for them.
